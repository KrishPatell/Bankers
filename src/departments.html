What medical services do you provide?

We offer a comprehensive range of specialized medical services tailored to meet your healthcare needs. Our departments include Knee Pain, Varicose Vein, Prostate, Piles, Frozen Shoulder, Heel Pain, Tennis Elbow, Vascular Malformation, and Tendo-Achillitis. Explore our services to experience quality care delivered by our expert team.

How can I make an appointment?

You can make an appointment by visiting our appointment page and filling out the form, or you can use our contact info to contact us during business hours.

What are your office hours?

Our office hours are as follows: Mon - Tue: 09:00 AM - 07:00 PM, Wed - Thu: 09:00 AM - 07:00 PM, Fri - Sat: 09:00 AM - 07:00 PM, Sunday: Closed.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, our hospital accepts insurance to facilitate comprehensive healthcare coverage for our patients.
