Genicular Artery Embolization

Knee pain and osteoarthritis are common problems that can affect daily life and mobility. As age increases, inflammation inside the knee joint may lead to pain, stiffness, swelling, and difficulty walking.Genicular Artery Embolization (GAE) is an advanced non-surgical knee pain treatment available at Bankers Vascular Centre Ahmedabad & Vadodara. This minimally invasive procedure helps reduce inflammation and relieve chronic knee pain without major surgery or knee replacement.Patients suffering from knee osteoarthritis, joint stiffness, swelling, or difficulty walking may benefit from this advanced image-guided treatment by Dr. Mohal Banker and team.
  • Non-Surgical Knee Pain Treatment
  • No Major Cuts or Stitches
  • Minimal Hospital Stay
  • Alternative to Knee Replacement Surgery

Varicose Vein Treatment

Varicose veins are enlarged, twisted veins commonly seen in the legs due to poor blood circulation and increased pressure inside the veins. Common symptoms include leg pain, swelling, heaviness, burning sensation, cramps, skin discoloration, and visible bulging veins.At Bankers Vascular Centre Ahmedabad & Vadodara, advanced non-surgical varicose vein treatment is available using minimally invasive techniques like Venaseal Glue Treatment and Laser Treatment. These modern procedures help treat varicose veins with minimal pain, faster recovery, and without major surgery.Patients suffering from leg pain, swollen veins, itching, heaviness, or visible varicose veins may benefit from early treatment to avoid complications.
  • Non-Surgical Varicose Vein Treatment
  • No Major Cuts or Stitches
  • Faster Recovery
  • Minimal Hospital Stay
  • Advanced Venaseal Glue & Laser Treatment

Prostatic Artery Embolization

Prostatic Artery Embolization (PAE) is an advanced minimally invasive treatment used to relieve urinary symptoms caused by an enlarged prostate (BPH). Common symptoms include frequent urination, difficulty passing urine, weak urine flow, nighttime urination, and incomplete bladder emptying.At Bankers Vascular Centre Ahmedabad & Vadodara, PAE treatment is performed using advanced image-guided technology without major surgery. This non-surgical prostate treatment helps reduce prostate size and improve urinary flow with minimal pain and faster recovery.Patients suffering from enlarged prostate symptoms may benefit from early treatment to avoid complications and improve quality of life.
  • Non-Surgical Enlarged Prostate Treatment
  • No Major Cuts or Stitches
  • Faster Recovery
  • Minimal Hospital Stay
  • Advanced Image-Guided Procedure

Hemorrhoidal Artery Embolization

Hemorrhoidal Artery Embolization (HAE) is an advanced minimally invasive treatment for piles (hemorrhoids) without major surgery. Piles are swollen veins in the rectal area that may cause pain, bleeding during bowel movements, itching, swelling, and discomfort while sitting.At Bankers Vascular Centre Ahmedabad & Vadodara, HAE treatment is performed using advanced image-guided technology to reduce blood flow to hemorrhoids, helping relieve symptoms naturally without cuts or stitches. This non-surgical piles treatment offers faster recovery with minimal pain and hospital stay.Patients suffering from bleeding piles, painful hemorrhoids, itching, or recurrent piles problems may benefit from early treatment to avoid complications.
  • Non-Surgical Piles Treatment
  • No Major Cuts or Stitches
  • Faster Recovery
  • Minimal Hospital Stay
  • Advanced Image-Guided Procedure

Uterine Artery Embolization

Uterine Artery Embolization (UAE) is an advanced minimally invasive treatment for uterine fibroids without major surgery. Uterine fibroids are non-cancerous growths in the uterus that may cause heavy periods, pelvic pain, abdominal swelling, frequent urination, and discomfort in daily life.At Bankers Vascular Centre Ahmedabad & Vadodara, UAE treatment is performed using advanced image-guided technology to block the blood supply to fibroids, helping them shrink naturally without removing the uterus. This non-surgical fibroid treatment offers faster recovery and minimal hospital stay.Women suffering from fibroids, heavy bleeding, pelvic pressure, or lower abdominal pain may benefit from early treatment to avoid complications.
  • Non-Surgical Fibroid Treatment
  • No Major Cuts or Stitches
  • Faster Recovery
  • Minimal Hospital Stay
  • Advanced Image-Guided Procedure
