Cancellation & Refund Policy


Last updated on May 6th, 2024
‍

Bankers Vascular Centre believes in maintaining transparency and patient satisfaction. We understand that circumstances may change, and therefore, we follow a fair cancellation and refund policy.

Cancellations

Appointment or procedure cancellations will be accepted only if requested within 2 days of booking.
However, cancellation requests may not be entertained if the consultation or procedure date is already scheduled and confirmed by our team or the treating doctor.
For any medical services or diagnostic tests already performed, no cancellation or refund will be applicable.

Refunds

Refunds will be considered only in genuine cases such as:Duplicate payment made by the patient.Service not rendered due to unavoidable reasons (e.g., doctor unavailability, rescheduling issues, or medical contraindications).Refund requests must be made in writing to our Customer Support Team within 2 days of the scheduled appointment.Once approved by Bankers Vascular Centre, refunds will be processed within 3–5 business days to the original mode of payment.

Non-Refundable Services

Registration fees, consultation charges, and procedure booking fees are non-refundable once the consultation or procedure has been availed.No refund is applicable for partially used services or treatment packages.
No refund is applicable for partially used services or treatment packages.

Quality & Complaints

If you are dissatisfied with the consultation or have concerns about the quality of service, please contact our support team within 2 days of your appointment.The management will review your concern and take an appropriate decision on a case-by-case basis.

Disclaimer

We use cookies to remember preferences and analyze site usage. You can control cookies through browser settings; disabling cookies may impact functionality.

At Bankers Vascular Hospital, we specialize in advanced, non-surgical treatments for various diseases, combining care with targeted therapy to provide personalized solutions for a wide range of vascular & joint diseases
